Who we are

Opportunity should not depend on access to the right room.

Founded in 2022, Genxyzra Next Generation Economic Development Corporation (“GENXYZRA”) works to help socially and economically disadvantaged individuals turn entrepreneurial goals into practical next steps.

Our model combines education, mentorship, resource navigation, and strategic partnerships to make economic development support more accessible.

Meet your digital guide

Every business begins with a conversation.
